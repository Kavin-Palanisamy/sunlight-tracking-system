## Hardware Description

### Microcontroller

The microcontroller receives the LDR sensor readings, compares the light intensity, and controls the motor according to the detected sunlight direction.

### LDR Sensor

The LDR is used to detect changes in light intensity. Different sensor readings help determine the direction of stronger sunlight.

### Motor

The motor changes the orientation of the solar panel according to the control signal from the microcontroller.

### Solar Panel

The solar panel converts sunlight into electrical energy.

### Power Supply

The power supply provides the required electrical power to the microcontroller, sensors, and motor.
